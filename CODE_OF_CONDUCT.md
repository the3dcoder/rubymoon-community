# Code of conduct

Everyone here is making things for small children, so let's treat each other the way we'd want them treated.

- **Be kind.** Assume the other person means well. Disagree about the work, never about the person.
- **Be patient.** Many people here are parents, not programmers, and plenty are new to GitHub. Every question is
  a fair question.
- **Review gently.** Say what you like as well as what needs changing, and explain why.
- **Keep personal details out.** Yours, your children's and everyone else's. No photos of children, ever.
- **No harassment, insults or unwelcome attention,** here or anywhere else, towards anyone involved.

If someone isn't following this, report it privately with the **Report a vulnerability** button on the
**Security** tab (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Only the reviewers see it.

Reviewers may edit or hide comments, close pull requests, withdraw packs and block people who don't follow this
code. If an author breaks it badly, every pack they've shared may be withdrawn.
